Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

Introduction

CarLog is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our vehicle management application.

Our Promise: We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share your information with third parties without your explicit consent.

Data Storage and Security

Cloud Storage

Your vehicle data is stored securely in Supabase cloud servers located in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1 region). This ensures:

  • Data residency within India as per local regulations
  • Fast access and synchronization across your devices
  • Enterprise-grade security and encryption
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery

Local Storage

A copy of your data is cached locally on your device for:

  • Offline access to your vehicle records
  • Faster app performance and responsiveness
  • Reduced data usage and battery consumption

Data Collection and Usage

CarLog collects only the information necessary to provide our vehicle management services:

  • Vehicle Information: Make, model, registration number, purchase date
  • Service Records: Maintenance history, service dates, costs
  • Insurance Details: Policy numbers, renewal dates, premium amounts
  • PUC Information: Certificate numbers, expiry dates
  • Fuel Logs: Fuel expenses, mileage data, fill-up dates
  • Documents: Photos of receipts, insurance papers, PUC certificates
Important: All documents and photos are accessible only by you, the account owner. We use secure authentication to ensure your data remains private.

Payments and Financial Information

CarLog uses Razorpay for payment processing. When you subscribe to our premium service:

  • Payment transactions are processed securely by Razorpay
  • CarLog does not store your credit card, debit card, or bank details
  • We only store transaction confirmation and subscription status
  • All payment data is encrypted and handled according to PCI DSS standards

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Razorpay for payment processing, Supabase for cloud storage
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfer: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
No Advertising: CarLog is a paid application. We do not show ads and do not use your data for advertising purposes.

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data anytime within the app
  • Export: Download your data as PDF reports
  • Correction: Update or modify any incorrect information
  • Deletion: Delete your entire account and all associated data

Account Deletion

To delete all your data:

  1. Open CarLog app
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Confirm your identity and deletion request
  4. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
Note: Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Please export any important information before requesting deletion.

Data Security Measures

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Authentication: Secure login and session management
  • Access Controls: Role-based access to sensitive data
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing
  • Compliance: Adherence to Indian data protection regulations

Contact and Support

If you have questions about this privacy policy or need help with your data:

  • Email: support@carlog.app
  • Response Time: We typically respond within 24-48 hours
  • Support Channels: Email support only for security and privacy reasons

For data-related requests or concerns, please include "Privacy Request" in your email subject line.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email to registered users
  • Updated effective date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of CarLog after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
